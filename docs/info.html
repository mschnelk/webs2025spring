Before You Book

  • When booking through the link, keep in mind that it is only a request form. I do not have the capacity to take all request and only want to accept clients who see eye to eye with me style-wise
  • Flash is preferred. You can find a more updated version of my flash on my instagram, and I have a hand-drawn flash book at my studio that you can flip through
  • Customs are always welcome. I've been enjoying medium-bigger and more complex pieces so please share your ideas. Requests sent with more photos and details are more likely to be accepted.
  • Custom designs will be shared at the appointment and adjustments can be made then if necessary
  • My average price range is around $160-$250. Price will ultimatley depend on the size, placement, flash/custom, and design adjustments.
  • A $50 deposit is required when booking.

Updates Regarding Small Tattoos

Currently, I only offer tattoos smaller than 2in if you get more than one (or as an add on to a bigger flash/custom piece)
I don't believe that you should be paying $150 for something of that size, but I also need to be realistic with myself and my business. I rely on tattooing for a solid part of my income, and I can't sustain myself with charging and doing such small pieces
Also, my style is always expanding. I'll always love doing smaller, find line pieces, but I don't want to limit myself to just that.
As such, I'm planning to reserve most of my small pieces for pop-ups or flash days.
Please keep this in mind as you're filling out a request form.

Cancellation/Rescheduling Policy

Cancellation within 24 hours of the appointment will result in a forfeit of your $50 deposit.

Rescheduling within 24 hours will require an additional $50 deposit.
