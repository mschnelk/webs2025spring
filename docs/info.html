info

before you book

Hi! Thank you for wanting a tattoo from me! And wanting to co-create some beautiful art! I like to think of my tattooing process as much more than just an appointment, I love to create a relaxing and comfortable environment, highlighting your agency and voice as a client, while also getting to know you! When you book through the link, please keep in mind that it's only a request form, as I don't have the capacity to take all requests and only want to take on clients who see eye to eye with me style-wise, since that is when I produce my best work where both you and I are proud of the piece!!
My current range/average price is around $160-$250. Price will ultimately depend on size, placement, flash/custom, and design adjustments. I require a $50 deposit when booking. Notice of rescheduling/cancellation within 24hrs of your appointment will require keeping the deposit or a second $50 if rescheduling.
As of right now, I will be offering flash pieces that have not already been tattooed shown in my "FLASH" highlights on my instagram AND some flash pieces from older posts.
**if you are interested in getting a flash piece and want something changed or added, I will make occasional adjustments, please indicate what you have in mind!**
Customs are always welcome! I have been enjoying medium-bigger and more complex pieces so please share your ideas! Custom designs will be shared at your appointment. Adjustments can be made then if necessary.

small tattoos

Currently, I only offer tattoos smaller than 2in if you get more than one (or as an add on to a bigger flash/custom piece)
I don't believe that you should be paying $150 for something of that size, but I also need to be realistic with myself and my business. I rely on tattooing for a solid part of my income, and I can't sustain myself with charging and doing such small pieces
Also, my style is always expanding. I'll always love doing smaller, find line pieces, but I don't want to limit myself to just that.
Please keep this in mind as you're filling out a request form.
